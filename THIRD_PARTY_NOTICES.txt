THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL AND EXTERNAL DEPENDENCIES — VERSION 1.0

This archive contains project-specific research artifacts and five Python scripts.
It does not bundle Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, model implementations or weights,
or original COCO/Flickr image pixels. The project's licenses do not replace the
licenses of those external components.

- Python 3 and its standard library: https://www.python.org/psf/license/
